Terms & Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection & Privacy

By registering for Vortex 14.0, you consent to the collection of your personal information (Name, Email, Phone Number, College Name, and College ID). We use this data strictly for:

  • Verifying your identity on the day of the event.
  • Sending important event updates and schedules via email or WhatsApp.
  • Generating your certificates of participation or merit.

Your data is securely stored and will never be sold to third-party marketers. It is only accessible by the Vortex organizing committee and core faculty.

2. Event Rules & Code of Conduct

Participants must carry a valid physical College ID card to enter the ICT Mumbai campus. The organizing committee reserves the right to disqualify any participant or team found violating event rules, engaging in malpractice, or causing damage to campus property.

3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

All registration fees are final. Refunds will only be initiated if an event is officially cancelled by the Vortex 14.0 organizing committee. If a participant fails to show up or is disqualified, no refund will be provided.

4. Media Consent

By attending the festival, you consent to being photographed or recorded. Vortex 14.0 reserves the right to use these media assets for future promotions and social media content.
